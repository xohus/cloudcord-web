CloudCord PC Bridge

BetterDiscord: copy CloudCordBridge.plugin.js into the BetterDiscord plugins folder.
Vencord/Equicord: copy the vencord folder into src/userplugins/cloudCordBridge and rebuild the client.

Includes Fake Profile synchronization, BotCord entry points and basic named badges. Full CloudCord overlays and native integrations remain exclusive to CloudCord.
