Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about CloudCord.

Is CloudCord safe to use?

The official CloudCord client is safe when obtained from getcloudcord.com or the official Xohus repository. It is not designed to steal credentials or tokens, provide remote access, mine cryptocurrency, or install unrelated software. Its client source can be inspected through the official repository and SourceVault under the CloudCord Proprietary Source License. Third-party plugins are separate software, so CloudCord cannot guarantee their safety; review a plugin and its permissions before enabling it.

Will this affect my primary Discord installation?

No. CloudCord operates autonomously and does not destructively patch or alter your primary host installation files in a way that breaks standard updates.

How do I access the plugins?

Over 450+ plugins are packaged directly within the CloudCord settings menu. You can toggle, configure, and override them natively without requiring external downloads.

What is the status of CloudCord on Windows (PC)?

🔴 Current Status: DOWN (Under Maintenance). Windows PC downloads and updates are temporarily offline while our developers investigate client compatibility updates. Mobile platforms (iOS & Android) remain fully available.

How do I install CloudCord on iOS?

Download the .ipa file and sideload it to your device using your preferred signing tool. We recommend the following methods:

  • ESign - A powerful on-device signing tool
  • Scarlet - An easy-to-use on-device installer
  • KSign - For seamless app signing
  • TrollStore - For permanent installation (if on a supported iOS version)
  • AltStore or Sideloadly - For computer-based sideloading

How do I install CloudCord on Android?

Download the .apk file from our download section. Open the file on your device to begin the installation. If prompted, make sure to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your Android settings.

Can my account get banned?

Modified clients may conflict with Discord's Terms of Service and can carry account-enforcement risk. CloudCord cannot guarantee that an account will never be limited or banned. Use a secondary account when evaluating client modifications, protect your credentials, install only trusted plugins, and review Discord's current policies before continuing.

Is getcloudcord.com the official website?

Yes. getcloudcord.com is the canonical website for CloudCord by Xohus. Authorized users can inspect the client source through the official repository and SourceVault under its proprietary license. This project is unrelated to CloudCord.net, cloudcord.io, and github.com/cloudcord, which identify a separate bot-hosting project.

Will CloudCord ask for my Discord password or token?

The official website does not ask you to type your Discord password or paste a Discord user token. When community verification is enabled, authorization is handled on Discord's own OAuth page and shows the permissions being requested. Never enter credentials into a page pretending to be Discord or send a token to a plugin, staff member, or support message.
