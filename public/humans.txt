CloudCord by Xohus
Official website: https://getcloudcord.com/
Client source: https://github.com/xohus/cloudcord
Security: https://getcloudcord.com/security

CloudCord by Xohus is a proprietary source-available Discord client customization project for iOS, iPadOS, Android, and Windows. The website is proprietary and is not open source. It is unrelated to CloudCord.net, cloudcord.io, and github.com/cloudcord.
