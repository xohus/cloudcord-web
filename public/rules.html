Community Rules

Rules for the official CloudCord Discord community

1. Respect other members

Harassment, threats, discrimination, targeted hostility, impersonation, and deliberate disruption are prohibited. Disagreements must remain civil.

2. Do not spam or advertise

Do not flood channels, repeatedly mention users, post unsolicited promotions, manipulate reactions, or evade channel restrictions. Advertising requires prior staff approval.

3. Keep the community safe

Malware, phishing, token stealers, credential collection, IP grabbers, destructive plugins, malicious links, and instructions intended to compromise another person are prohibited.

4. Protect private information

Do not publish passwords, Discord tokens, license keys, addresses, private conversations, personal records, or other sensitive information. CloudCord staff will never request your Discord password or raw account token.

5. Use channels correctly

Post support requests, bug reports, plugins, themes, suggestions, and general discussion in their designated channels. Search for an existing answer or report before creating a duplicate.

6. Share content responsibly

Only share code, plugins, themes, artwork, and files you are authorized to distribute. Credit original creators and clearly identify third-party work. Paid, private, leaked, or stolen content is prohibited.

7. No moderation evasion

Do not use alternate accounts or technical workarounds to evade warnings, timeouts, removals, bans, verification, or other moderation actions.

8. Follow platform and applicable rules

Members must follow Discord's Terms and Community Guidelines and may not use CloudCord or this community to facilitate unlawful conduct, platform abuse, harassment, or unauthorized access.

9. Cooperate with staff

Follow reasonable staff instructions. Moderation decisions may be appealed respectfully through the designated process. Public harassment of reporters, witnesses, or staff is prohibited.

10. Enforcement

Staff may remove content and apply warnings, timeouts, role restrictions, removals, or bans based on severity, history, and community safety. Serious security threats may result in immediate removal.

Use of CloudCord is also governed by the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
